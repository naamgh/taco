Mobile concept

Tap a day → detail docks below

The grid stays clean — each day shows only a colour dot (done) or a dashed ring (planned). Tapping a day fills the empty space beneath with its detail: a preview of the workout for a past day, or an add-a-plan prompt for a future one. Tap around the calendar below — it's live.

How it reads

  • Past, done — tap Oct 6, 4, 14… a preview card appears: thumbnail, name, zone, duration, TSS, RPE. Tapping it would open the full detail.
  • Future, empty — tap Oct 20+ → “Plan a workout”, with quick type chips.
  • Future, planned — tap Oct 23 → the planned workout with a Start/Edit.
  • Past, empty — tap Oct 7 → “Rest day”.
  • Days with two activities (e.g. Oct 11) stack two cards.
