How it behaves

  • Both — +/- moves Work and Rec together. Identical to the current intensity control; nothing changes for existing use.
  • Work — +/- trims only the efforts. The recovery targets stay where they are.
  • Rec — +/- trims only the recoveries. Your case: bump recovery watts without touching the hard intervals.

The Work 100% · Rec 100% line is the source of truth — both values are always visible, the active one lit. Steps are ±1%, no bounds (recovery can even exceed work).
