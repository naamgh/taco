TIER 2

Adaptive training plan

plan that reacts

A plan lays workouts into your calendar with progression, recovery weeks and a taper. The adaptive part is what makes it feel like a coach: it watches your actual Form and completion, and re-flows upcoming sessions when life (or fatigue) gets in the way.

⟳

Adapted today. Your Form dropped to −28 after the weekend block — deeper than planned. I eased Thursday's VO2 Max → 45 min Endurance, and moved the hard session to Saturday when you'll be fresher. Your week's target load is unchanged.

Triggers I'd react to: Form below the plan's floor → downgrade the next hard day · a workout you couldn't hold → repeat it easier · missed sessions → re-flow, don't cram · fresher than expected → offer to add intensity.
NOTE

How it stays "your app"

Everything above is deterministic — plain rules over data you already have (Form, load, drift, the library). It runs offline on the trainer, costs nothing, and never leaves the device. An optional conversational AI coach could sit on top later, but it's not needed to ship any of this.
